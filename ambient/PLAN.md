# Lantern Rain Room — plan (from 2026-11-23)

Goal: a calm, trusted sleep/study ambience channel. Growth comes from search and from people replaying
the same video every night, so every long video must be a different, carefully made scene.

## Weekly rhythm
- 3–4 long videos per week (1 hour for now), each a NEW scene with its own sound mix.
- 1–2 Shorts from every long video (40 s seamless loops, no on-screen text, small brand tag only),
  each linking to its long video in the first description line.
- Daily Zapier budget (~5 uploads, shared with Zeipan and Brain Warp Lab): ambience gets 1 long + 1 Short
  on long-video days, 1–2 Shorts on the other days.

## Christmas season (user, 2026-11-24): December is Christmas on this channel
Christmas ambience goes ONLY to Lantern Rain Room (never to Brain Warp Lab or Zeipan — channels stay separate).
Each video is ONE sound world (fire, snow or rain — never mixed, no wind, no music), 1 hour, plus 1–2 Shorts.
1. Christmas Fireplace — crackling fire only; tree lights twinkle, quiet snow in the window
   (ambient/christmas-fireplace.json, img/christmas-fireplace.jpg) — rendering 11-24
2. Snowy Christmas Village — soft snow hush; heavy slow snowfall over lit cottages (image 11-24)
3. Rainy Christmas Window — rain on glass; fairy lights inside, blurred city tree outside (image 11-25,
   ElevenLabs free plan allows only a few images per day; Canva credits were used up on 11-24)
4. Christmas Eve bedroom / snowy cabin porch / Christmas train window — next, one per 2–3 days
Titles lead with the search phrase: "Christmas Fireplace", "Christmas Snowfall", "Christmas Rain Sounds".

## Scene list (one per long video, in this order)
1. Cozy cabin — rain + fireplace (done, uploads tonight)
2. Snowy cabin window — blizzard wind + fireplace
3. Forest tent — rain on canvas + distant thunder
4. Rainy Paris café — rain on awning + soft café murmur (no music)
5. Library at night — rain on tall windows + clock ticking + fire
6. Seaside cottage — ocean waves + rain
7. Japanese room — rain on the veranda + wind chimes
8. Train at night — rain on the window + train rumble
9. Thunderstorm over a lake house — heavy rain + rolling thunder
10. Winter fireplace only — crackling fire (no rain)
Then repeat the best performers with new pictures and new mixes, never copies.

## Every video, every time
- Picture: Canva (free daily images) or ElevenLabs images; animated parts (fire, lanterns) with
  tools/ambient_loop.py.
- Sound: ElevenLabs SFX v3 seamless loops (2 per layer), mixed with tools/ambient_layers.py.
- Audio QA from POSTING.md before upload (peaks, clicks, tones, level steadiness).
- SEO: keyword-first title, keywords in the first two description lines, 3 hashtags, tags.
